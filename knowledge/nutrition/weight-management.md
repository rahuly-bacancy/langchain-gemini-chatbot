# Nutrition for Weight Management

## Energy Balance
Body weight is influenced by energy intake and energy expenditure over time. Sustainable weight management generally benefits from a moderate, maintainable calorie deficit rather than extreme restriction.

## Meal Structure
Build meals around vegetables or fruit, a protein source, high-fiber carbohydrates or whole grains, and appropriate amounts of healthy fats.

## Practical Habits
- Prefer minimally processed foods most of the time.
- Include vegetables and fruit regularly.
- Choose water or low-calorie beverages frequently.
- Pay attention to portion sizes.
- Keep highly processed, energy-dense foods as occasional choices rather than the foundation of the diet.

## Sustainability
Rapid or highly restrictive diets can be difficult to maintain. A gradual approach with realistic habits is generally more practical.
