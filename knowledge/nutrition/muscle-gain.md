# Nutrition for Muscle Gain

## Principle
Muscle gain requires resistance training plus adequate energy and protein intake.

## Food Pattern
Include protein-rich foods at regular meals, carbohydrates to support training, fruits and vegetables for micronutrients and fiber, and dietary fats in appropriate amounts.

## Calorie Intake
A small, controlled energy surplus may support muscle gain while limiting unnecessary fat gain. The appropriate amount depends on the individual and should be monitored over time.

## Monitoring
Track body weight trends, training performance, recovery, and appetite. Adjust food intake gradually rather than making large changes.
