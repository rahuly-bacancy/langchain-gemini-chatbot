# Protein and Fitness

## Role
Protein supplies amino acids needed for tissue maintenance and repair and supports adaptation to resistance training.

## Food Sources
Animal sources include eggs, dairy, fish, poultry, and meat. Plant sources include soy foods, beans, lentils, peas, nuts, seeds, and whole grains.

## Practical Approach
Distribute protein-containing foods across meals rather than relying on one very large serving. For people training regularly, total daily protein intake and overall dietary adequacy matter more than a single post-workout meal.

## Vegetarian Options
Useful options include tofu, tempeh, soy milk, Greek yogurt or other dairy products, lentils, beans, chickpeas, and paneer.

## Safety
Individual protein needs vary with body size, activity, goals, age, and health status. People with kidney disease or other medical conditions should discuss protein intake with a qualified clinician.
