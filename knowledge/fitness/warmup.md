# Warm-Up and Exercise Preparation

## General Warm-Up
A warm-up can gradually increase body temperature and prepare the body for activity. Examples include brisk walking, cycling, or other easy aerobic movement.

## Specific Warm-Up
Before a strength exercise, perform lighter practice sets of the movement and progressively approach the working load.

## Mobility
Use controlled movement through comfortable ranges. Mobility work should not force painful positions.

## Cool-Down
A gradual reduction in activity can be used after exercise. Stretching is optional and should be comfortable rather than painful.
