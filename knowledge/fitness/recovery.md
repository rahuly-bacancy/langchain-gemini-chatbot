# Exercise Recovery

## Sleep
Adequate sleep supports physical and mental recovery. Consistent sleep habits are useful for people who exercise regularly.

## Rest
Recovery needs depend on training volume, intensity, fitness level, age, stress, and sleep. Avoid repeatedly training the same muscles hard when they have not recovered.

## Hydration
Drink fluids regularly and increase attention to hydration during prolonged exercise or hot conditions.

## Warning Signs
Persistent unusual fatigue, dizziness, fainting, chest pain, severe shortness of breath, or significant pain should not be treated as normal training discomfort. Stop exercise and seek appropriate medical evaluation when necessary.
