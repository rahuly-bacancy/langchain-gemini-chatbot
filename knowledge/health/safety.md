# Fitness and Health Safety

## Scope
This knowledge base provides general educational fitness and nutrition information. It is not a diagnosis or a substitute for medical care.

## When Professional Advice Is Important
Seek advice from an appropriate qualified healthcare professional for known medical conditions, significant injuries, pregnancy, eating disorders, unexplained symptoms, or questions about medication and exercise interactions.

## Emergency Symptoms
Severe chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, fainting, signs of stroke, or other potentially life-threatening symptoms require urgent medical attention rather than a chatbot workout recommendation.

## Exercise Safety
Start at an appropriate level, learn technique, progress gradually, and stop activities that cause acute or unusual pain.
