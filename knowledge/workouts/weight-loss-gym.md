# Gym Training for Weight Management

## Principle
Resistance training can help maintain or build muscle while a person works toward weight loss. Combining resistance training with regular aerobic activity can support overall fitness.

## Example Weekly Structure
- Day 1: Full body resistance training
- Day 2: Moderate aerobic activity
- Day 3: Full body resistance training
- Day 4: Rest or light activity
- Day 5: Full body resistance training
- Day 6: Moderate aerobic activity
- Day 7: Rest

## Resistance Session
Choose major movement patterns such as squat, hinge, push, pull, and core exercises. Use a load that permits controlled technique.

## Important Note
A workout plan should be adjusted for training experience, available equipment, schedule, injuries, and medical restrictions.
