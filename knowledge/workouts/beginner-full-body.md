# Beginner Full-Body Workout

## Purpose
A beginner-friendly resistance-training template for general fitness. Start with manageable loads and prioritize technique.

## Session
- Squat or goblet squat: 2-3 sets of 8-12 reps
- Push-up or machine chest press: 2-3 sets of 8-12 reps
- Seated row or dumbbell row: 2-3 sets of 8-12 reps
- Romanian deadlift with light weight: 2-3 sets of 8-12 reps
- Plank: 2-3 sets of 20-40 seconds

## Rest
Rest about 1-3 minutes between sets, using more rest when needed to maintain good technique.

## Progression
When all prescribed repetitions can be completed with good form and the exercise feels manageable, increase resistance gradually. Avoid large jumps in load.

## Safety
Stop an exercise if it causes sharp or unusual pain. Beginners should consider professional instruction when learning unfamiliar exercises.
