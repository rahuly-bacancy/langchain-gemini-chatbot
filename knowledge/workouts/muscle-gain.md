# Resistance Training for Muscle Growth

## Principle
Muscle growth is supported by progressive resistance training, adequate recovery, and sufficient nutrition.

## Exercise Selection
Use a combination of compound and isolation exercises that train major muscle groups. Examples include squats, presses, rows, pulldowns, hinges, curls, triceps exercises, and calf exercises.

## Training Approach
Use challenging sets with controlled technique. A broad moderate-repetition range can be effective; the exact number of sets and repetitions should be individualized.

## Progression
Track exercises, resistance, repetitions, and perceived effort. Gradually increase training demands while maintaining good form.

## Recovery
Allow adequate recovery between hard sessions and prioritize sleep and adequate food intake.
